About Stackly

Three cars, one parking lot, and a stubborn idea.

In 2018 we bet that renting a car shouldn't feel like a chore. Eight years, 42 cities and a very patient maintenance crew later, that bet is still paying off — one freshly-inspected vehicle at a time.

Our mission

Make every rental feel like your own car.

No queues, no haggling over scratches that were already there. Just a clean, inspected vehicle waiting exactly where and when you need it — booked from your phone in the time it takes to make coffee.

02

Our vision

A city where nobody needs to own a car.

We're building the fleet, the charging network and the trust it takes for a subscription to fully replace a driveway. Fewer idle cars, cleaner streets, and a ride ready for you whenever the plan changes.

The road so far

Our journey, one milestone at a time

2018

Three cars, one lot

Stackly opens in a single Chennai parking lot with three hatchbacks and a shared spreadsheet.

2019

Ten cities, one app

The booking app ships. Instant approval replaces paperwork, and the fleet crosses 300 vehicles.

2021

Going electric

Our first EV hatchbacks join the fleet alongside a fast-charge partner network in six cities.

2023

1,000+ vehicles

Luxury sedans and SUVs join the lineup as the fleet crosses a thousand vehicles nationwide.

2026

42 cities and counting

Today Stackly runs a self-serviced fleet across 42 cities — and we're just getting started.
